Privacy Policy

The short version

Simplicalc collects no personal data and sends nothing off your device. Everything stays on your phone. That's what "fast & private" means.

What information does Simplicalc store?

Simplicalc stores the following information locally on your device only, using Android's standard local storage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server, third party, or developer.

  • App settings — your chosen color palette, accent color, key style, layout preferences, and lettering options.
  • Calculation history — only if you explicitly enable the history feature in Settings. History is off by default. You can clear it or disable it at any time.
  • Unlock state — whether you have unlocked special themes or features through in-app interactions.

This information exists solely to remember your preferences between sessions. It is stored on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

What information does Simplicalc NOT collect?

  • No personal information (name, email, phone number, age)
  • No location data
  • No camera or microphone access
  • No contacts or files
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No crash reporting or diagnostic data sent externally
  • No account or sign-in required

Does Simplicalc share any data?

No. Simplicalc does not share, sell, trade, or transmit any data — because it does not collect any data to begin with. There are no third-party SDKs, analytics services, or advertising networks integrated into this app.

Internet access

Simplicalc performs all calculations locally on your device. No internet connection is required or used for the calculator's core functionality. The app does not make any network requests during normal use.

Children's privacy

Simplicalc does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected, the app is safe for users of all ages.

Data retention & deletion

All locally stored data (settings, history, unlock state) is stored only on your device. You can clear your calculation history at any time from within the app (Settings → Privacy & History → Clear all). Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data permanently.
